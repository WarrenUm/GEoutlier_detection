# Supplemental reading — ARMA Models in statsmodels

Curated outside resources for **Topic 38: Time Series Models**. Use these to go beyond the
lesson README: read a paper for depth, watch a video for intuition, and skim
an article or another lesson for a second explanation.

## Scientific papers

- [Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control (Box & Jenkins)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781118619193)
  — The Box-Jenkins methodology that defines ARIMA modeling.

## Videos

- [Time Series Analysis (ritvikmath ARIMA series)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DeORzP0go5I)
  — Clear video series on AR, MA, ARMA, and ARIMA.

## Articles

- [A Gentle Introduction to ARIMA (ML Mastery)](https://machinelearningmastery.com/arima-for-time-series-forecasting-with-python/)
  — Article on stationarity, ACF/PACF, and fitting ARIMA in Python.

## Other lessons & docs

- [Forecasting: Principles and Practice (Hyndman, free online)](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)
  — The standard open text for time-series decomposition and ARIMA.
- [statsmodels — Time Series Analysis (tsa docs)](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/tsa.html)
  — Reference for ACF/PACF, ARIMA, and SARIMAX.
