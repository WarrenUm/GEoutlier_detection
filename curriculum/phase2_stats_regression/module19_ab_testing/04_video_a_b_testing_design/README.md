In this lesson hypothesis testing is explored further in the application of A/B testing. The topics covered in this lecture include: the relationship between hypothesis testing and A/B testing, the considerations in designing an A/B test, how to conduct an A/B test, experimental design, the novelty effect, change aversion, effect size, power, power analysis, control group, experimental group

The repository for this lecture can be found here - [A/B Testing: Design](https://github.com/flatiron-school/ds-ab_test_intro-nbz32)
